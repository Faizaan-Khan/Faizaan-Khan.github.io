Doremon

Doremon is a personal assistant that Faizaan Khan built for his own use. It runs on his own computer and talks to him through a private Telegram bot.

It reads his Gmail, Google Calendar and his own task spreadsheet so it can tell him what needs his attention each day. It does not send email, change his calendar or share his data with anyone without his explicit approval.

Doremon is not a public service, has no other users, and is not available for sign-up.
